About Premium Agricultural Products Uganda.

Founded in 2018, Premium Uganda Agricultural Products is a leading agricultural producer of many Agricultural products based in Kampala city with branches all over Uganda. We specialised in growing and distributing high quality agricultural products both locally and internationally.

Our farms utilize modern, sustainable farming techniques combined with traditional uganda agricultural kowledge to produce exceptional yields year-round.

We are committed to supporting local farmers through our cooperative model that provides training, fair wages, and access to international markets.
